Nothing in the inbox?

Work down these three. The last one is the one that catches people out, and no amount of resending fixes it.

1

It hasn't turned up

Mail is queued, not instant. Give it a couple of minutes, then search your inbox for LearnFRC rather than scrolling, because a new message doesn't always land at the top.

2

The link says expired

Links last an hour and open once. If you asked twice, only the newest one works. Come back here and send another, the dead one stays dead.

3

It's a Google account

Then there's no password on the account to reset and no email is coming. Use Continue with Google on the sign-in sheet and you're in.

note on accuracy

LearnFRC is in beta. Lessons are AI-assisted: drafted from primary sources like the WPILib docs, the game manual and vendor sites, then reviewed for accuracy. If something looks wrong, open the lesson and suggest an edit. Every open suggestion is public on the contributions page, and every fix we make to a fact is written down in the corrections log.
